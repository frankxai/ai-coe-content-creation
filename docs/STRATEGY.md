# Starlight Intelligence Publishing Engine — Strategy

## Executive decision

Build **ebooks and podcasts as two renderers of one living, versioned knowledge system**. Do not run separate editorial pipelines and do not optimize for audiobook royalties as the primary business.

The category position is:

> Starlight is the definitive intelligence layer for human-controlled agentic work.

The durable promise is not “latest AI news.” It is:

> What changed → which architectural layer changed → what to implement → what it costs → how to verify it.

The screenshots that initiated this plan show a crowded beginner/tutorial category, repetitive “clearly explained” positioning, weak commodity covers, and long synthetic audiobooks with little executable evidence. The whitespace is operator-grade synthesis tied to tested repositories, decision models, cost data, and update guarantees.

Do not invent a generic “AI Institute” brand or new domain. **Starlight Intelligence Institute** is distinct, extensible, and compounds the existing entity.

### Attribution rules

- A flagship guide uses Frank as author only after he approves the thesis, consequential claims, original framework, examples, and final edition.
- Fast research deltas may use **“Starlight Intelligence Research Desk, edited by Frank Riemer.”**
- Narrator and author are separate metadata fields.
- Synthetic narration is disclosed. A voice clone is never represented as spontaneous live speech.
- Sponsorship never changes tool rankings, benchmark outcomes, or conclusions.
- Vendor product names remain independent editorial topics; include a trademark-independence note where appropriate.

## One research object, six commercial surfaces

Each topic begins as an edition object rather than a manuscript.

1. **Evidence graph** — atomic claims, primary sources, snapshots, tests, counterclaims, and freshness class.
2. **Canonical living guide** — ungated, searchable, cited, versioned, and continuously updated.
3. **Edition package** — PDF, EPUB, diagrams, glossary, changelog, source notes.
4. **Audio package** — adapted spoken script, chapter audio, transcript, VTT/SRT, show notes, clips.
5. **Operator pack** — repository, skills, prompts, checklists, architecture templates, evaluation pack.
6. **Academy module** — exercises, evidence portfolio, credential, workshop, and team license.

Ebook prose is not narrated verbatim by default. The spoken adaptation resolves diagrams and tables, removes raw URLs, expands acronyms, and preserves claim identities without citation clutter.

## Three-speed editorial system

| Clock | Product | Length | Strategic role | Default voice |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| 24–48 hours | Release Delta | 800–1,500 words; 6–12 min | Capture material changes and immediate search demand | Persistent synthetic research voice |
| Weekly | Living Guide update + briefing | 2,500–6,000 words; 15–35 min | Build topical authority and returning audience | Frank intro/outro; premium synthetic body |
| Quarterly | Frozen operator edition/report | 20,000–45,000 words; 2–4 h | Monetizable durable synthesis | Frank human or tightly directed owned clone |
| Annual | Definitive field guide/audiobook | 45,000–80,000 words | Category-defining IP and licensing anchor | Frank human for highest-value passages |

A minor product change generates an audio delta; it does not force a full audiobook rerecord. The living web edition advances continuously. Retail audiobook editions are frozen and versioned.

### Brian is not a brand asset

ElevenLabs **Brian** is a legacy Default voice. It is unavailable to newer accounts and retires after the end of 2026. ElevenLabs suggests **Sawyer — Midnight Storyteller**, but that is a migration aid, not a reason to build Starlight identity on a stock voice.

Use Brian only as a short benchmark if the current account can access it. Build the durable identity from either:

1. an owned, consented Frank clone;
2. an owned Voice Design / Professional Voice Clone persona for Mira; or
3. a contractually stable licensed narrator.

### Voice selection experiment

Run a blinded 12-sample benchmark before choosing the persistent voice.

- Four script types: release delta, technical explanation, narrative thesis, dialogue.
- Three candidate providers: ElevenLabs, xAI/Grok, Speechify or Gemini.
- Score naturalness, authority, technical pronunciation, fatigue after 10 minutes, edit stability, cost, latency, and rights durability.
- Twenty listeners are sufficient for the pilot when at least five are target buyers.
- Select one female research persona and one Frank-clone pathway; do not rotate voices by episode.

### Subscription decision

Start with:

- **ElevenLabs paid commercial plan or pay-as-you-go** for the premium benchmark and one persistent voice.
- **xAI API pay-as-you-go** for inexpensive conversational and delta experiments.
- **Speechify API Starter only when it wins the bulk blind test**; do not buy the consumer reader subscription for production.
- Existing OpenAI/Gemini access as fallback and batch test surfaces.
- Deepgram or an equivalent ASR API for round-trip QA.

Do not hold three overlapping creator subscriptions indefinitely. After the 30-day benchmark, retain one premium narrator, one inexpensive batch/realtime fallback, and one ASR/QC provider.

### URL contract

- /guides/ owns evergreen definition and implementation.
- /comparisons/ owns product and architectural decisions.
- /briefs/YYYY-MM-/ owns dated change analysis.
- /reports/ owns original research and benchmarks.
- /books/ owns editions, downloads, and purchase intent.
- Episode pages own a dated conversation or are integrated into the canonical guide.
- Thin tag, filter, and archive pages are noindex.
- Substantive updates change sitemap lastmod and trigger IndexNow; cosmetic edits do not.
- FrankX and social surfaces publish excerpts, not mirrored full text.

### Canonical guide standard

Every flagship guide includes:

- answer-first opening;
- original thesis and decision model;
- at least three original diagrams;
- at least two tested workflows or executable artifacts;
- cost and ROI model;
- failure modes and recovery;
- primary-source references;
- visible version, source cutoff, and changelog;
- author, editor/reviewer, rights, and correction policy;
- companion episode, transcript, repository, and operator pack.

Mass-generated pages are prohibited. Automation improves research, verification, transforms, and freshness; it does not manufacture information gain.

| Quarterly content system | €2,500–8,000 | variable | One 12-seat cohort at €997 |

Break-even units equal:

**(production cost + update reserve) / (price × net contribution margin)**

A €900 operator bundle at €129 and 85% contribution breaks even at approximately nine sales.

Expected year-one revenue mix:

- 40–50% Academy and cohorts;
- 20–25% operator library/manuals;
- 15–20% team licensing;
- 10–15% affiliates and sponsors;
- under 10% ebook/audiobook retail royalties.

## Execution roadmap

### Days 0–14 — prove the loop

Milestone: one field guide, one episode, one public page, one owned feed.

- Lock category, entity names, byline, and one canonical query.
- Create edition, claim, rights, and release schemas.
- Build two official-source ingestors and immutable source snapshots.
- Produce a verified research pack for Graph Engineering.
- Author the pilot guide through a pull request.
- Generate spoken adaptation; blind-test three voice stacks.
- Render web, EPUB, PDF, transcript, and audio.
- Implement approval waitpoint and release-manifest hash.
- Publish R2 enclosure and owned RSS.
- Submit the feed manually to Spotify, Apple, and YouTube.
- Instrument content IDs, cost events, and release receipts.
- Execute one rollback drill.

Out of scope: multilingual, Frank voice clone, retail audiobook, commerce, custom mobile app, and automated social posting.

### Days 15–30 — establish the show and offer

- Publish three canonical living guides and four to eight episodes.
- Select Mira and the premium provider from the blind test.
- Launch Agent Loop Engineering preorder at €79–129.
- Add Search Console, IndexNow, RSS health, completion, and subscriber dashboards.
- Prove one source-change → stale claim → partial rerender → republish cycle.
- Create FrankX excerpt distribution and one email capture pathway.
- Review every subscription and retire redundant voice tools.

Exit gate: one content system produces qualified subscribers and at least one purchase signal.

### Days 31–90 — compound authority

- Reach 8–12 canonical guides and at least 12 episodes.
- Release two ebook/audio bundles and three operator packs.
- Launch Operator Library beta and first Agentic Workbench Sprint.
- Publish State of Agentic Work report with an original benchmark.
- Build query ledger, internal-link engine, controlled atomization, and freshness scoring.
- Add second TTS adapter only after the first provider's limits are measured.
- Integrate commerce/private feed and attribution.
- Secure first aligned sponsor only after editorial charter is public.

Exit gate: source-change-to-publication SLA below 48 hours and a credible route to €5,000 monthly attributable revenue.

### Days 91–365 — become the institution

- Maintain 12–15 canonical guides and quarterly update contracts.
- Publish weekly briefings, monthly deep dives, quarterly State reports, and two flagship audiobooks.
- Launch team licensing and AI Architect Academy modules.
- Expose selected research/claim pages and downloadable datasets.
- Add multilingual editions only where query and conversion data prove demand.
- Add professional podcast hosting/IAB analytics only when sponsor revenue justifies it.
- Kill or merge any cluster that fails to generate 100 qualified subscribers or meaningful downstream revenue within two quarters.

## Goals and metrics

### North star

**1,000 opted-in people who repeatedly consume, implement, and recommend the corpus.**

### Search authority

- weighted share of top-3, top-10, and top-20 positions across governed queries;
- non-branded impressions, clicks, CTR, and conversion by cluster;
- brand-search growth for Frank and Starlight;
- time to index and valid indexed / submitted canonical pages;
- backlink and citation velocity from reports, datasets, and tools;
- answer-engine citations where measurable.

### Content and platform quality

- 30/90-day returning reader/listener rate;
- audio start, completion, and chapter drop-off;
- Spotify reach → interest → consumption conversion;
- Apple engaged listeners and average consumption;
- YouTube search impressions, watch time, retention, and returning viewers;
- transcript and technical-pronunciation error rate;
- verified-claim freshness and source-change-to-update latency.

### Commercial performance

- qualified opt-in rate;
- ebook download → subscriber conversion;
- operator manual and library conversion;
- GitHub stars, forks, and template activations;
- cohort and team-license pipeline;
- affiliate revenue per query cluster;
- revenue, cost, and human review time per edition object.

## Non-negotiable gates

- One canonical URL and content graph per topic.
- No factual assertion without verified claim binding.
- No “latest” claim without a freshness owner and review SLA.
- No audio release without narrator rights, disclosure, and a stored rights manifest.
- No clone presented as spontaneous human speech.
- No publication without an immutable release manifest and exact human approval.
- No derivative that introduces a new factual claim.
- No sponsor influence on verdicts.
- No flagship guide without original evidence, diagrams, workflows, cost model, failure modes, and changelog.
- No new distribution channel without stable IDs, receipts, and measurable conversion.
